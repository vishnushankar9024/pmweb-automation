1.          INTRODUCTION

Purpose of the Questionnaire

The purpose of this pre-qualification questionnaire is to enable organisations to provide the University of Leeds and their appointed Consultant Team with sufficient information to assess their capability and suitability, for inclusion for the Invitation to Tender for this project. The questionnaire has been structured to encompass a general assessment of capability and suitability, supplemented by relevant experience, financial standing and technical ability.

Notes for Completion

  • Please answer all questions as fully as possible, continuing on separate sheets where necessary. Mandatory information is required for Sections A, B and E. Failure to provide this information will result in invalidation of the submission.

 

  • Any question that does not apply to your organisation or your organisation is unable to provide a response please enter either ‘Not Applicable’ or ‘Response not given’.

 

  • The provision of false or misrepresented information could result in your organisation’s exclusion from the bidding process.

 

  • In this questionnaire the term ‘organisation’ refers to Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Incorporated Company, Co-operative etc. as appropriate.

 

  • All enquiries are to be made to Mr Colin Challinor through the University electronic in-tend system. Responses and questions from any other source or party will not be acknowledged.

 

  • The questionnaire must be submitted electronically, a Hard copy may be called in the event of the requirement to clarify any points. All responses must be received by the University of Leeds by 12 noon on *******.

 

  • The Client will not be responsible for, or pay any costs or expenses which are incurred by, any Tenderer in preparing and submitting the pre-qualification questionnaire, or in visiting the site in connection therewith.
